# 6F.2 — historical block reversion

Two unrestricted Global Replay runs (seeds 42/43, one epoch, rank/alpha 32, Muon .00045, native W8A8, 22 swaps, validation seed 42, same 15-image dataset and caches). Capture raw FP32 A/B factors immediately before update 4 (three completed updates), probe immediately before update 12 (eleven completed updates). Eight actual optimizer updates separate the snapshots. No resume or historical optimizer state restoration is performed. Ordinary training resumes unmodified after exact registered-state rollback; no policy or production changes.

Predeclared endpoints: all 28 single-block full reversions, all-block early checkpoint, half factor interpolation for blocks 13/17/26, and six uniform all-block interpolations matched to the measured FP32 effective-weight movement of each focus block at full/half reversion. 38 endpoints in each of two diagnostic precisions. Matching is independent of target loss; actual committed movement must agree within 0.1%. Factor interpolation is not necessarily linear in effective-weight space. Record per-case target gain and prediction/base displacement, FP32/BF16 geometry, hashes, exact restoration after each endpoint, repeated block26 with reversed input order, and final restoration after precision switch. Keep incomplete JSON on failure; the comparison ZIP contains both reports. Retain historical factors only in RAM until the probe finishes (about 410 MiB additional FP32 snapshot at rank 32; extra parent copy during probe). The full scan is substantially slower than 6C.2k-D.

Interpretation: an improvement upon reversion is local counterfactual evidence about accumulated block changes in the presence of all other later blocks, not proof of individual causal learning over the interval or justification to freeze permanently. Compare full/half focus reversions to their movement-matched controls; report both seeds and both evaluators separately, and treat the 28-block scan as exploratory. Native W8A8 and weight-only are not FP32 ground truth. Holdouts are the same two development target images; no independent semantic preservation panel or samples. No progression to 6C.4 or 7-series without review.
