# Experiment 5B — causal block identity
Five epochs, 145 optimizer updates, seed 42, LR 0.00045, same 29 training / 3 holdout dataset, Muon and strict deterministic settings from 5A. Test Mode queues eight arms in order: unrestricted, adaptive gradient, count-matched random, exact mask replay, block 26 alone, block 13 alone, unrestricted with decay 10.0, unrestricted with decay 30.0. The four interventions load ONLY the completed adaptive source in the current queue's report. They keep the same warmup and full-block probes, bypass adaptive scoring, and use source probe counts/identities or the block-26 suspension schedule. B5 and B6 have equal omission timing/count; neither is required to match B2's overall count. All arms use explicit identical Muon settings except the two decay controls. Source failure prevents dependent arms from proceeding.
All arms log raw factor fingerprints at every step, actual gradient-bearing masks, per-factor probe telemetry, raw and EMA validation each epoch, and unchanged preview prompts. Full FP32 raw/EMA checkpoints at each epoch retained locally (~34.4 GB across eight arms, excluding ordinary saves/states). The ZIP still excludes weights. Report validates all expected masks and records source-vs-control first hash and mask differences. Exact replay must match B2 at every step; if not, investigate before claiming causality. Also compare B5/B6 directly, including their shared prefix. Hashes establish adapter-factor equality, not optimizer-state equality. Determinism established only for 58 steps in 5A; recheck full horizon. The single seed is exploratory, not independent replication. Production defaults unchanged.

## Added regularization controls
B7/B8 train every block with decoupled weight decay 10.0 and 30.0, versus B1's 0.01. No change in LR, routing, initialization, optimizer momentum or evaluation. At a constant 0.00045 for 145 updates, pure decay would retain about 27.0% and 1.9% of the effective BA contribution, respectively, if both factors were shrunk without gradient updates. These are illustrative shrink-only estimates, not predicted final adapter norms. Actual optimizer rates and decay are logged at every step. Compare both raw/EMA validation and preview preservation against B1 and selective arms. Factor decay is not freezing: Muon continues updating both factors. Decay controls differ intentionally from step 1 and are excluded from mask-prefix causal diagnostics; their own report comparison is against B1. The added arms do not replace a later matched effective-displacement or LR calibration experiment. All eight arms retain FP32 checkpoints locally.

## Expanded probe telemetry (fourth research review)
B7/B8 now use decay 10 and 30. Requeue any previously queued 5B recipe; the old decay-1 arm is not part of this experiment. Every arm records the original total update RMS plus separate theoretical decoupled decay RMS and reconstructed Muon update RMS, using each parameter's actual pre-step group LR and decay and skipping decay on absent gradients. The decomposition has rounding residuals and RMS components are not additive. Each probe also measures post-clip gradient vs pre-step momentum cosine over the concatenated eligible factors for each block and A/B type, with counts of eligible and gradient-bearing matrices. Missing or zero old momentum is null, not zero confidence. No optimizer state is created or changed by telemetry. CPU transfers/reductions add unmeasured overhead; no continuous controller or optimizer change is introduced. Exact-mask replay remains the full-horizon neutrality check for routing. Cross-version comparison with 5A's strict hashes through step 58 is a useful additional diagnostic. Compute effective BA norms and checkpoint-to-checkpoint displacement offline after 5B, with rank-sized Gram matrices. Keep the checkpoint files locally.
