# Experiment 5B — causal block identity
Five epochs, 145 optimizer updates, seed 42, LR 0.00045, same 29 training / 3 holdout dataset, Muon and strict deterministic settings from 5A. Test Mode queues eight arms in order: unrestricted, adaptive gradient, count-matched random, exact mask replay, block 26 alone, block 13 alone, unrestricted with decay 1.0, unrestricted with decay 10.0. The four interventions load ONLY the completed adaptive source in the current queue's report. They keep the same warmup and full-block probes, bypass adaptive scoring, and use source probe counts/identities or the block-26 suspension schedule. B5 and B6 have equal omission timing/count; neither is required to match B2's overall count. All arms use explicit identical Muon settings except the two decay controls. Source failure prevents dependent arms from proceeding.
All arms log raw factor fingerprints at every step, actual gradient-bearing masks, per-factor probe telemetry, raw and EMA validation each epoch, and unchanged preview prompts. Full FP32 raw/EMA checkpoints at each epoch retained locally (~34.4 GB across eight arms, excluding ordinary saves/states). The ZIP still excludes weights. Report validates all expected masks and records source-vs-control first hash and mask differences. Exact replay must match B2 at every step; if not, investigate before claiming causality. Also compare B5/B6 directly, including their shared prefix. Hashes establish adapter-factor equality, not optimizer-state equality. Determinism established only for 58 steps in 5A; recheck full horizon. The single seed is exploratory, not independent replication. Production defaults unchanged.

## Added regularization controls
B7/B8 train every block with decoupled weight decay 1.0 and 10.0, versus B1's 0.01. No change in LR, routing, initialization, optimizer momentum or evaluation. At a constant 0.00045 for 145 updates, pure decay would retain about 87.8% and 27.1% of the effective BA contribution, respectively, if both factors were shrunk without gradient updates. These are illustrative shrink-only estimates, not predicted final adapter norms. Actual optimizer rates and decay are logged at every step. Compare both raw/EMA validation and preview preservation against B1 and selective arms. Factor decay is not freezing: Muon continues updating both factors. Decay controls differ intentionally from step 1 and are excluded from mask-prefix causal diagnostics; their own report comparison is against B1. The added arms do not replace a later matched effective-displacement or LR calibration experiment. All eight arms retain FP32 checkpoints locally.
